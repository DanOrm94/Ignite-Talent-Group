Policy publishing rule

Public pages should give short, readable explanations and link to the approved current PDFs. Do not publish internal procedures, PQQ answer banks, evidence trackers, live fact-check schedules, candidate documents, training matrices, certificate copies, insurance schedules or claims history, incident/complaint/breach/whistleblowing logs, or supplier due-diligence files.

Before production launch

  1. Each vertical lead signs off services, role families, locations and named contacts.
  2. Nick confirms which entity contracts, employs and controls candidate data for each website.
  3. Dan connects client and candidate actions to approved forms or inboxes and adds concise privacy wording.
  4. The compliance owner selects only approved, current documents from the controlled Drive folder.
  5. Any accreditation, insurance, membership or numerical claim is checked against current evidence immediately before publication.
  6. Cookie consent, analytics configuration, keyboard access, screen-reader structure, form handling and responsive behaviour are tested on the production build.
