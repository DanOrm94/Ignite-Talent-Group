Ignite Construction website – design and compliance brief

Direction for a clean, credible construction recruitment website, with clear and controlled compliance information.

Purpose and design direction

The site should feel clean, modern and corporate, while demonstrating that Ignite Construction is a properly structured and compliant construction recruitment and labour-supply business rather than simply a standard recruitment landing page.

The design should be sharp and relatively minimal: strong Ignite branding, clear navigation, good use of white space, professional construction imagery, and clear calls to action for both clients and candidates.

From a procurement perspective, someone reviewing Ignite during a PQQ, PSL or supplier-approval process should be able to identify the compliance position quickly.

Proposed website structure

  • Home / Construction
  • About Ignite Construction
  • Clients / Employers
  • Candidates
  • Sectors / Disciplines
  • Compliance & Accreditations
  • Policies
  • Contact

Policies & Compliance

We have a substantial suite of company policies and compliance documents. Rather than placing large amounts of policy wording directly on the website, the preference is for a clean Policies & Compliance page, similar to the accompanying HTML concept, with clear tiles or buttons linking to the current approved documents.

Where possible, links should point to centrally controlled versions in Google Drive. The current policy-document folder is: Ignite Construction policies – Google Drive. This allows the underlying document to be updated centrally when a policy is reviewed, without rebuilding the website page.

The policy suite includes:

  • Health & Safety Policy
  • Environmental & Sustainability Policy
  • Quality Management Policy
  • Modern Slavery & Labour Exploitation Statement
  • Right to Work & Candidate Vetting Policy
  • Data Protection Statement / Privacy Policy
  • Equal Opportunities / EDI Policy
  • Ethical Recruitment Policy
  • Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy
  • Supplier / Umbrella / Labour Supply Code
  • Training & Competence Statement
  • Construction Labour Supply Compliance Statement
  • Carbon Reduction Plan

Content responsibility: Use the supplied Google Drive policy folder as the controlled document source. Policy content does not need to be recreated for the website.

Compliance and accreditations

Main compliance credentials should be incorporated visually where appropriate, rather than placed solely in the footer. A simple accreditation/compliance strip could work well on the Home, Clients and Compliance pages.

  • SMAS accreditation
  • CHAS / VeriforceONE status
  • Living Wage accreditation / employer mark
  • ICO registration
  • GDPR and data-protection controls
  • Employers' Liability insurance
  • Public Liability insurance
  • Professional Indemnity insurance
  • Health & Safety support through HS Direct
  • Internal staff compliance training
  • Right to Work and candidate-vetting procedures

Publication control: Do not display an accreditation as final if its certificate or status is still being completed. Exact wording and status for each item will be confirmed before publication.

Data protection and website forms

Candidate registration, CV-upload and client-enquiry forms should include appropriate privacy wording and a link to the Privacy/Data Protection documentation. The forms should remain concise rather than carrying several paragraphs of GDPR wording.

Suggested wording: “By submitting this form you acknowledge that your personal information will be processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy.” The Privacy Policy should link to the controlled document.

Client message

Ignite Construction provides compliant, professionally managed construction recruitment and labour-supply services, supported by robust health & safety, candidate vetting, insurance, training, data-protection and ESG controls.

This does not need to appear word-for-word, but it is the position that the design and content should communicate.

Overall appearance and next steps

  • Corporate, clean, sharp and credible.
  • Modern without being overly stylised.
  • Clearly construction-focused.
  • Easy to navigate on desktop and mobile.
  • Suitable for both candidates and major construction clients.

The compliance information needs to be prominent enough to support procurement and PQQ enquiries without overwhelming the recruitment side of the site.

The accompanying Policies & Compliance HTML file is a visual and content starting point, rather than something that needs to be reproduced exactly. Please feel free to improve the layout and presentation within the overall Ignite brand.
